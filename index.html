Our Journey

From a Santa Fe startup to a statewide give-back

StudentSpace was built by people who believed small colleges deserved the same data tools as the big ones. Here's how a for-profit software company became a resource for New Mexico's next generation of edtech builders.

1998

Founded in Santa Fe

StudentSpace opens its doors on the premise that small colleges (1,000 to 5,000 students) deserve high-touch, affordable data tools rather than enterprise systems built for massive research universities.

1998–2020s

Built for 250+ institutions

Full Circle Tracking, SchoolView, and our assessment tools go on to serve colleges and universities across the country — built in partnership with research grounded in work with the U.S. Department of Education.

Ongoing

A give-back that started small

Long before this relaunch, the StudentSpace Foundation was already providing free access to our tools for underfunded schools and low-income families across New Mexico and California.

2026

The full transformation

We're retiring the license fees and the sales pitch. edplan.ai launches as a free planning portal for New Mexico students, and every product we've built becomes free, open material for New Mexico entrepreneurs to build on.

A free planning portal for every student in New Mexico

edplan.ai is our answer to a simple question: what if every student in the state — not just the ones at well-resourced schools — had a clear, guided way to plan their path through education and into a career?

Built with, not just for, New Mexico

edplan.ai draws on the same student-tracking expertise StudentSpace has spent 27 years refining for colleges — turned toward helping individual students plan their own course, from middle school through career, with no cost to students, families or schools.

Full Circle Tracking

Connects a campus's student information system, CRM, and LMS into one cohesive advising view, so student success teams see the whole student — not fragments spread across silos.

What the product does

In typical colleges and universities, critical student indicators are trapped in separate databases: grades in the SIS, interactions in a CRM, course engagement in the LMS, and financial holds in the bursar's system. Full Circle Tracking aggregates and normalizes these disparate streams in near real-time, giving retention specialists and advisors an early-warning radar for students at risk of dropping out.

Who on campus uses it

  • ✓Academic Advisors: For holistic pre-session reviews, milestone tracking, and cross-department advising notes.
  • ✓Deans of Retention & Student Success: To identify institutional drop-off trends and manage cohort-based retention campaigns.
  • ✓Registrars & Financial Aid Officers: To track prerequisite verifications, course completion ratios, and satisfactory academic progress (SAP).

What transfers to NM builders

When you apply for Full Circle Tracking through the Give-Back Program, you receive the full commercial asset package:

Source Code Full application source repository, data synchronization daemons, and normalized schema.
Architecture Technical specifications, SIS integration adapters (Banner, Colleague, PeopleSoft), and security docs.
Playbook The exact commercial sales decks, RFP templates, pricing calculators, and contract frameworks used across 250+ campuses.
Mentorship Direct code walkthrough and technical onboarding with StudentSpace's software engineers.

SchoolView

An enrollment, advancement, and operations dashboard engineered specifically for small colleges and specialized institutions without large IT departments.

What the product does

Small colleges often lack the seven-figure IT budgets and dedicated business intelligence teams required to build custom operational dashboards. SchoolView delivers an out-of-the-box command center that unifies admissions pipelines, tuition yield tracking, donor advancement, and daily institutional health metrics into accessible, executive-ready views.

Who on campus uses it

  • ✓College Presidents & Provosts: To monitor real-time enrollment numbers, net tuition revenue targets, and institutional vitality metrics.
  • ✓Admissions Directors: For funnel conversion analysis, high-school territory performance, and yield forecasting.
  • ✓Advancement & Alumni Relations: To manage capital campaign progress, alumni giving velocity, and donor segment engagement.

What transfers to NM builders

Everything required to deploy, rebrand, and sell SchoolView to small colleges and regional universities:

Source Code Complete web dashboard application codebase, reporting queries, and role-based access control modules.
Architecture Data warehouse schema, ETL scripts, and cloud deployment container configurations.
Playbook Small-institution sales cycle playbooks, demo scripts, customer objection handling guides, and procurement roadmaps.
Mentorship Orientation on higher-ed leadership purchasing dynamics and contract renewal structures.

Assessment of Student Learning (ASL)

A framework and toolset for measuring competency-based learning outcomes at the course, program, and department level.

What the product does

Accreditation agencies (HLC, SACSCOC, WSCUC, NECHE) mandate that higher education institutions demonstrate rigorous assessment of student learning outcomes. ASL bridges the gap between faculty rubrics and administrative compliance, automating the aggregation of assignment evaluations into department-level competency benchmarks without placing bureaucratic overhead on instructors.

Who on campus uses it

  • ✓Assessment Coordinators & Accreditation Liaisons: To generate audit-ready outcome evidence for regional and programmatic reviews.
  • ✓Department Chairs & Curriculum Committees: To identify curriculum gaps, adjust course sequences, and substantiate academic quality improvements.
  • ✓Instructional Faculty: To apply standardized assessment rubrics directly to course assignments and final capstones.

What transfers to NM builders

A proven academic assessment framework with immense market demand across accredited institutions:

Source Code Outcome scoring engine, rubric aggregation software, and compliance reporting generators.
Architecture Outcome-mapping taxonomies, LMS assignment hook specifications, and accreditation export schemas.
Playbook Higher-ed accreditation marketing guides, provost presentation decks, and multi-year subscription pricing models.
Mentorship Deep briefing on navigating faculty senate approvals and university assessment committee evaluations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Give-Back Program FAQ

Everything you need to know about receiving StudentSpace source code, product designs, and business playbooks.

Do I need an existing registered business to apply?

No. Individuals, informal teams, students, and early-stage founders based in New Mexico are all fully eligible. If you haven't formed a legal entity yet, you can still apply and be accepted. Our business playbook materials even outline best practices for incorporating a technology company in New Mexico.

Are non-technical applicants eligible?

Yes. While maintaining and extending software ultimately requires software development skills, non-technical founders with deep educational, advising, or sales experience can apply. We provide comprehensive architectural documentation and technical onboarding sessions to help you or a technical partner understand the system architecture from day one.

Is there an application deadline?

No. The Give-Back Program operates on a rolling admissions basis. We review incoming applications continuously so that New Mexico builders can begin when they are ready, rather than waiting for an arbitrary calendar window.

[Legal to confirm exact license terms]
The underlying principle of the transfer is to empower New Mexico recipients to commercialize, rebrand, adapt, and build viable independent businesses around the software without ongoing royalty fees or equity encumbrance from StudentSpace. The exact formal license agreement will be provided prior to repository transfer.

Does StudentSpace take any equity or royalties from my company?

No cost. No equity. No catch. StudentSpace takes zero percent equity in your venture and charges zero licensing or royalty fees. This initiative is funded through StudentSpace's CSR commitment as a tribute to the state that nurtured our company for 27 years.

Can I modify, rebrand, and resell the software?

Yes. You are expected to rebrand the software, develop new features, adapt the user interface to modern expectations, and sell it under your own company's identity to colleges, universities, K-12 systems, or state agencies.
